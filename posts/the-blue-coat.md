# The Blue Coat

Bringing it back to February 2025, when I traveled through Japan for almost a month with a very good friend. Not my first time there (went for a karate trip to watch the world championship, that's a different story).

Our first few days were about rediscovering Tokyo. On Harajuku day, saw a bunch of vintage stores. One place, I can still remember, was below ground, and had a massive indoor and outdoor selection. It was freezing outside (Japan in the winter can be pretty cold and don't be surprised to find yourself sick for a week, stuck in a hostel in Kyoto), so looking at the coat selection made sense. Two caught my attention: a khaki/orange one and another blue. I didn't know which one to buy so decided to get both. Maybe because of how annoying it was to get them in and out of the suitcase, I just wore the orange one the entire time, basically every day. Only when I came back home and the weather was right, I decided it's time to pull out the blue coat as well.

I got some pretty good comments on it (and it's not like I'm considered much of a sharp dresser). Indeed a nice coat. But a thing that kept returning was a comment about the big unignorable patch on its front, that said "Todd". The first time I smiled. The second time I said it's my nickname. On the third I was like: "who is Todd?" It's a real person isn't it?

If he's real, how do I find him? I might just accept this quest.

The coat:

"Byrne Hollow Farm".

Well, hello there.

Based in New York, narrowed it down to about 125 employees.

Then I found an important detail, a label with the man's full name: "Todd Dambrosio".

![Label with Todd Dambrosio's name](imgs/blue-coat/label.jpg)

Ok so it's company swag, and this "Aramark" is a known manufacturer of such.

## How do you find a person?

I'm definitely not a surveillance or an OSINT (Open-Source Intelligence) expert, though back in the day we used to play a game where you point at a stranger and compete to find him online. Feeling pretty capable, I remember being at a basketball game and finding one of the cheerleaders in just a couple of minutes.

## From Byrne Dairy to Todd

From looking over their website, I saw these "Employee Spotlight" sections.

![Byrne Dairy Employee Spotlight](imgs/blue-coat/employee-spotlight.jpg)

I thought "ok let's just use Claude to crawl this site and look for Todd" - which got no results, except WordPress blocking me.

![WordPress blocking the crawl](imgs/blue-coat/wordpress-blocked.jpg)

Then I just searched online for any sign of Byrne's employee list.

Found this post on Facebook, I thought for a second I've found my truck driver and that Facebook is useful for other reasons than the marketplace. I'm sorry, no appreciation for you Todd B.

![Facebook search](imgs/blue-coat/facebook.jpg)

## Or is it D'ambrosio?

So the name is Italian, and the way they do it is with an apostrophe - D'ambrosio (as in "from the family of Ambrosio").\
Pivoting with the updated name, I was looking on social networks and this person showed up at the top, so I decided to try and contact him. Wrote this very cute message in my opinion, but unfortunately didn't get a reply.

![Message sent on Instagram](imgs/blue-coat/dambrosio-3.jpg)

There are these "find a person online" services, which surprisingly there are quite a lot of. First came this Todd, but soon understood that he's a Speech-Language Pathologist and also the same person as the one on Instagram…

![RocketReach result](imgs/blue-coat/dambrosio-1.jpg)

Later came this. Todd is that you?

![LeadIQ result](imgs/blue-coat/dambrosio-2.jpg)

Of course the details themselves are behind a paywall, and that's where I've drawn the line and stopped.

I'm sure there was a lot more I could have done and that I've only scratched the surface of this matter (for all the stalkers out there).

## Closing thoughts

Although empty handed, I think about this reunion between me and a stranger who makes milk for a living. Or maybe it's the reunion of the stranger who makes milk for a living and his coat?

Thinking about it all, the idea of modern intelligence can be unsettling. Once a nation backed operation or a movie like subject, is now a fun exercise. Fun but also destructive. In the back of our minds, we know that in our age, privacy may just be an illusion. Our presence is much more than physical and our identity is scattered all over the internet. A single person has by himself (especially now with the assistance of AI agents) the power way beyond one has ever had.\
As someone who works in Cyber Security for a living, I tend to underthink about these concerns in my personal life. Not because I dismiss what can happen, but because I want to protect myself from the unbearable life of constant anxiety.

When it starts raining again and I look at my coat section, maybe I'll have the urge to search for Todd again. Crawl the internet for redemption. In some ways I like it open ended. But I can also imagine him the way I want to, or just create him:
